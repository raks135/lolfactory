Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers LOL Factory and its upload tool, LOL Factory Uploader (“the app”). The app is operated by the owner of the LOL Factory YouTube channel for that one channel only.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app can upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, set their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times, check whether an upload has gone public, and post a comment on the channel's own videos. It requests these permissions: youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl.

What the app stores

  • A Google sign-in token for the channel owner's account, stored on the owner's private server and used only to run the actions above.
  • The videos and text the owner chooses to publish, and a log of what was posted and when.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube users.
  • It does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.
  • No person other than the channel owner has access to the sign-in token or the account data.

Google API Services

LOL Factory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the app then loses all access and the stored token stops working and is deleted. To ask about or remove any data, contact us below.

Third-party content

Clips are made from videos published under Creative Commons licences. Each post credits the original creator and links to the source video. If you are a creator and want a clip removed, contact us and we will take it down promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
